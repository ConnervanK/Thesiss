Based on the contents and structure of the workspace, the following major research topics and computational experiments have been explored:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Ground Penetrating Radar (GPR) Simulation \& Modeling:} 
    Building forward models using gprMax, incorporating complex geometries like subwavelength scatterers, thin beds, and baseline structural models.

    \item \textbf{Full Waveform Inversion (FWI):} 
    Developing FWI workflows (using tools such as Devito), experimenting with objective functions including quantum/geometric misfits, and employing optimization techniques like L-BFGS.

    \item \textbf{Time-Reversal \& Array Signal Processing:} 
    Implementing high-resolution imaging algorithms, specifically Time-Reversal MUSIC (TR-MUSIC), to analyze diffractions and localize small targets.

    \item \textbf{Reverse Time Migration (RTM):} 
    Processing synthetic B-scan data to produce migrated subsurface images.

    \item \textbf{Wave Scattering \& Diffraction Analysis:} 
    Conducting focused experiments on horizontal scattering phenomena, multi-transmitter setups, and subwavelength resolution limits.

    \item \textbf{Bayesian Inference \& Uncertainty Quantification:} 
    Applying Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) methods for probabilistic inversion and parameter estimation.
\end{itemize}
